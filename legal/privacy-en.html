Mola: Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026 · Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy explains what information the Mola game ("Mola", "the game") collects, why, and what choices you have. Mola is developed by Mustafa Kılıç ("we"). Contact: m_kilic@outlook.com.tr.

1. Short version

  • Your game progress is saved on your device.
  • Mola uses Unity Gaming Services (analytics). Analytics events are sent only if you choose "Allow" on the first launch screen. You can change this at any time in Settings > Data.
  • Unity's crash diagnostics are turned off: no crash dumps are collected. With your permission, the game sends only an error count and the first line of an error message as an analytics event.
  • If you choose "No", no analytics or error data is sent from the game.
  • This version has no ads, no in-app purchases, and no accounts.

3. Information we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.
  • No microphone or camera access.
  • No crash dumps, stack traces, or memory snapshots.
  • No advertising identifiers and no advertising profiles. This version has no ads.

4. Your choice

On first launch, you see a screen with two equal buttons: "Allow" and "No". Neither is selected by default. If you close the screen without choosing, it is treated as "No". You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Data. Turning it off stops sending data from the game.

5. Where data is processed

Data is processed by Unity Technologies on its servers. Unity Analytics data is stored at rest in Google Cloud europe-west4 (Netherlands); if Data Access is enabled, a copy may be replicated to us-central1 (United States). Customers cannot choose the storage region (Unity support article). This means data may leave your country. Such a transfer takes place only on the basis of your explicit consent (the "Allow" button on the first launch screen). If you choose "No", your data is not transferred.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your information and do not share it for advertising. Unity Technologies processes this data on our behalf as a service provider (data processor) under its terms.

7. How long data is kept

By default, Unity keeps personal data, including analytics events, for 13 months; raw event data is deleted automatically after that period. Dashboard metrics and user profiles are kept until deletion is requested or the developer removes them (sources: Unity Analytics FAQ; Unity privacy overview). Your game progress stays on your device until you delete the app, and may be included in Android's device backup if you use it. Analytics data is not included in backups.

8. Deleting your data

Turning off Data in Settings > Data stops collection, and raw data is deleted automatically after 13 months. To have earlier data deleted, email m_kilic@outlook.com.tr with the subject "Data deletion". The game does not display your installation ID, so we may ask for your device model and the approximate date you installed the game; if we cannot match the data to you, we will tell you. We will reply within a reasonable time. Requests for data held by Unity can also be made through Unity's own process.

9. Children

Mola is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent data through the game, email us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, where the change is significant, show the new terms in the game.
